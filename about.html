About Matcha Maya & Our Mission

We combine creative design, SEO expertise, and a passion for global connection to empower digital nomads and podcasters worldwide.

✍️ Author Bio & Team Experience

Founded by Dawid Millenium-Design, Matcha Maya was created to solve a key pain point in the remote work industry: fragmented, unreliable information for digital nomads, remote workers, and audio creators.

With years of hands-on experience in technical web design, organic search optimization, and global remote logistics, our research desk evaluates international hubs using verified metrics—from fiber internet reliability to ergonomics and cost-of-living breakdowns.

🎯 Why We Built Matcha Maya

Most location guides focus only on lifestyle photos. We built Matcha Maya to provide actionable, 12-point technical evaluations for every destination—giving creators the exact data needed to work, stream, and produce content smoothly overseas.

🎙️ Podcaster & Creator Focus

Beyond remote workspace logistics, we provide creators with structured podcast proposal templates, guest interview portals, and studio location maps across 220+ cities to streamline remote show production.
