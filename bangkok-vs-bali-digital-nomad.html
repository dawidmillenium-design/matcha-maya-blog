💡 Frequently Asked Questions (Bangkok vs Bali)

Which city is cheaper for digital nomads, Bangkok or Bali?

Living expenses in Bangkok average around $1,400/mo per month, compared to approximately $1,300/mo per month in Bali.

Does Bangkok or Bali have faster internet for remote work?

Bangkok offers average speeds of 120 Mbps, while Bali averages 85 Mbps.

What are the best coworking spots in Bangkok and Bali?

In Bangkok, a top recommended remote work spot is HUBBA Ekkamai. In Bali, remote workers frequently use Dojo Bali.
