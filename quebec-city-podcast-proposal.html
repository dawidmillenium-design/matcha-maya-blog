🍵 MATCHA MAYA Interviews Quebec City Nomad in Quebec City

Published: July 2026 · 8 min read · Video Call Interview

MATCHA MAYA is on a global mission to find the best coworking spaces, ergonomic furniture, and matcha experiences. In this video call interview, she connects with Quebec City Nomad (@quebeccity), a local influencer based in Quebec City, to talk about working remotely in a great city for digital nomads.

📹 The Video Call

"Quebec City Nomad, thanks for joining me! You're in Quebec City right now, working from Quebec City Coworking. How's the vibe there today?"

Quebec City Nomad: "It's vibrant and dynamic! Quebec City Coworking is one of the top coworking spaces in Quebec City. Today, it's full of creatives, entrepreneurs, and digital nomads."

"Let's start with the chair you're sitting on. What's the ergonomic situation like?"

Quebec City Nomad: "The chairs are comfortable ergonomic chair. I've been working here for hours and my back feels completely fine."

"Now, tell me about the desk setup—is it spacious enough for a dual-monitor workflow?"

Quebec City Nomad: "Absolutely! The desks are spacious well-designed desks with power outlets. There's plenty of room for a dual-monitor setup and even some personal items."

"One of the most important things for remote work is a good internet connection. What's the average internet speed in Quebec City? Is the Wi-Fi here fast and reliable?"

Quebec City Nomad: "Quebec City has 50-80 Mbps (100 Mbps at coworking). It's more than enough for video calls, streaming, and large file uploads."

"Now, the most important question: what matcha drink and cake are you sipping and snacking on right now?"

Quebec City Nomad: "I'm having a Matcha latte and a slice of Quebec City specialty cake. It's a perfect pairing!"

"What makes Quebec City a great place for digital nomads?"

Quebec City Nomad: "Quebec City is a city of incredible energy and opportunity. The cost of living is affordable, the food is amazing, and there's a huge community of remote workers."

"This has been so insightful! Where can people follow your work?"

Quebec City Nomad: "Follow me on Instagram at @quebeccity, where I share the best of Quebec City."

🍵 MATCHA MAYA's Final Thoughts on Quebec City

Quebec City is a city of incredible energy and opportunity. Quebec City Coworking offers a vibrant coworking space with comfortable ergonomic chair, spacious well-designed desks with power outlets, and fast internet. The Matcha latte and Quebec City specialty cake is a must-try. While flights to Dubai are more expensive than three years ago, Quebec City's affordability and energy make it a top choice for digital nomads.
