🍵 MATCHA MAYA Interviews Louisville Nomad in Louisville

Published: July 2026 · 8 min read · Video Call Interview

MATCHA MAYA is on a global mission to find the best coworking spaces, ergonomic furniture, and matcha experiences. In this video call interview, she connects with Louisville Nomad (@louisville), a local influencer based in Louisville, to talk about working remotely in a great city for digital nomads.

📹 The Video Call

"Louisville Nomad, thanks for joining me! You're in Louisville right now, working from Louisville Coworking. How's the vibe there today?"

Louisville Nomad: "It's vibrant and dynamic! Louisville Coworking is one of the top coworking spaces in Louisville. Today, it's full of creatives, entrepreneurs, and digital nomads."

"Let's start with the chair you're sitting on. What's the ergonomic situation like?"

Louisville Nomad: "The chairs are comfortable ergonomic chair. I've been working here for hours and my back feels completely fine."

"Now, tell me about the desk setup—is it spacious enough for a dual-monitor workflow?"

Louisville Nomad: "Absolutely! The desks are spacious well-designed desks with power outlets. There's plenty of room for a dual-monitor setup and even some personal items."

"One of the most important things for remote work is a good internet connection. What's the average internet speed in Louisville? Is the Wi-Fi here fast and reliable?"

Louisville Nomad: "Louisville has 50-80 Mbps (100 Mbps at coworking). It's more than enough for video calls, streaming, and large file uploads."

"What makes Louisville a great place for digital nomads?"

Louisville Nomad: "Louisville is a city of incredible energy and opportunity. The cost of living is affordable, the food is amazing, and there's a huge community of remote workers."

🍵 MATCHA MAYA's Final Thoughts on Louisville

Louisville is a city of incredible energy and opportunity. Louisville Coworking offers a vibrant coworking space with comfortable ergonomic chair, spacious well-designed desks with power outlets, and fast internet. The Matcha latte and Louisville specialty cake is a must-try. While flights to Dubai are more expensive than three years ago, Louisville's affordability and energy make it a top choice for digital nomads.
