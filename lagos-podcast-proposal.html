Podcast Interview: 13 Key Local Questions Answered

Q1: How is the local internet infrastructure and what SIM cards work best for tourists?

Answer: Internet speeds routinely exceed 100 Mbps in established commercial neighborhoods across Lagos. Mobile connectivity is seamless, with local 5G tourist SIMs and global eSIM passes taking under 5 minutes to set up upon arrival.

Q2: How would you rate the quality of local influencer blogs, vlogs, and Instagram/TikTok accounts?

Answer: Content creators based in Lagos maintain informative travel and living guides online, making it easy for newcomers to discover top workspace spots and housing advice.

Q3: Are there local books, e-books, or courses covering internet business and local SEO?

Answer: Yes, central coworking hubs in Lagos frequently organize workshops, e-learning courses, and informal panel discussions on scaling online businesses internationally.

Q4: Are local politicians and government initiatives supportive of tech innovation?

Answer: Local authorities recognize the economic benefit of remote professionals, actively improving public Wi-Fi networks and long-term stay residence options across Lagos.

Q5: What is the cost of a 1-hour helicopter ride over the city skyline?

Answer: A private 1-hour scenic helicopter tour over Lagos typically ranges from $1,200 - $1,800 USD depending on group size, charter company, and seasonal demand.

Q6: Are offline English language schools easily accessible in central districts?

Answer: Finding qualified language instruction in Lagos is straightforward, with multiple language institutes located near central residential hubs.

Q7: What is the best time in the calendar year to visit Lagos?

Answer: The optimal window to visit Lagos is during November to April (Dry Season / Peak Nomad Window), offering clear skies, comfortable working temperatures, and peak social networking events for expats.

Q8: How does the rent for a 1-bedroom luxury apartment today compare to 3 years ago?

Answer: A modern 1-bedroom luxury apartment in central Lagos costs approximately $1,800 - $2,500 / mo today, compared to roughly $1,200 - $1,600 / mo three years ago due to increased global demand.

Q9: What does it cost to rent a room with a private bathroom in a big villa today vs 3 years ago?

Answer: Renting an en-suite room in a luxury shared villa currently costs $800 - $1,100 / mo, up from $500 - $750 / mo three years ago.

Q10: What is the typical cost of a party night out at the top disco club in town?

Answer: A night out including entry, drinks, or a shared VIP table at the premier nightlife venue in Lagos costs around $150 - $350 USD (VIP Table & Drinks).

Q11: What are the costs, numbers, accessibility, and opening hours for local rooftop bars?

Answer: Lagos features 15+ popular rooftop venues, cocktails $12 - $20 USD, open 5:00 PM - 2:00 AM, elevator accessible. Elevator access and handicap facilities are standard in major commercial tower venues.

Q12: How convenient is home food delivery and what local apps are used?

Answer: Food delivery services cover nearly all central residential districts in Lagos. Major regional platforms comprise GrabFood, Foodpanda & ShopeeFood (avg delivery fee $1.00 - $2.50 USD, 25 - 40 min delivery).

Q13: What are the most frequently connected domestic and international cities from the local airport?

Answer: Top domestic connection: Secondary regional hub & coastal islands. Top international routes: Singapore, Bangkok, Hong Kong & Dubai.
